IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint
The idea, in one line: Practice a high stakes conversation just before it happens and get the exact words to say
Why me: I have faced this situation multiple times

GOAL
The one goal they hire it for (money, time, status or life): Status (perception in different situations)
Delta 4 (the steps today → the steps with my product):
Today:

1. A high stakes conversation is coming up.
2. They think of asking a friend, but feel embarrassed to bring it up.
3. They record themselves having the conversation.
4. Save the recording.
5. Upload the recording to ChatGPT.
6. Get the feedback.
7. The feedback is vague and doesn't help with the details.
8. It doesn't drill them on what the other person might ask next.
9. They feel frustrated, don't rehearse, and walk in unprepared.

With my product:

1. Open the WhatsApp chat.
2. Pick the situation: ask for a raise, explain a delay, find your place at a party, know where we stand in dating.
3. The demo person speaks first (e.g. demo manager: "I have 2 mins before my next meeting, what are you asking for on pay?").
4. They answer with a voice note and get a score and a better version of their own words.
5. The demo person asks follow-ups until the overall score stays above 85.

USER
The trigger (when the pain hits): just before a high stakes conversation: a raise, a delay update, joining a room at a party, asking the person they're dating where this is going
Who: someone prepping for an important conversation they don't want to get wrong, with no one to rehearse with or too embarrassed to ask friends
Today's path, step by step: they ask ChatGPT or a friend, get vague advice, don't rehearse, and walk in unprepared
Who they trust on this decision: their own instinct, or a friend/someone they trust
Would they pay? (what exists today that people pay for): They would pay 49/- (spoke to 3-4 users). Nothing as such in India. Quippy is there in the US, but many people don't use it in India

PRODUCT
Onboarding (how a first-time user feels the value fastest): open the chat, pick a situation, answer the demo person's first question with one voice note, get a score and a better version. No login, no name.
The core loop (user stories): Just before a high stakes conversation, I want to communicate the right thing, so that I don't end up losing the conversation.
The AI-first part: scores the voice note on Clarity, Confidence, Persuasion and Warmth (each out of 100, overall = average), writes a better version of their own words, and asks follow-ups as the demo person until they pass 85
