# DESIGN.md
Read this before building or changing any message. This product lives in WhatsApp: every screen is a message. If a choice isn't covered here, ask me instead of guessing.

## 1. The feeling, in labels
- Intro: one short message that says what this does and lists the four situations to pick from.
- Scorecard: four scores (Clarity, Confidence, Charisma, Warmth) and the overall, readable in 3 seconds.
- Better version: their own words, rewritten so they can say it as-is in the real conversation.
- Follow-up: the demo person's next question, in character, one question only.

## 2. References, one per component
Intro: Instinct
Take: short, friendly, one clear choice
Ignore: long welcome text, menus inside menus
Demo person: Instinct (a personal agent on WhatsApp that chats like a friend)
Take: sounds like a real person, not a bot
Ignore: everything else about Instinct
Scorecard: 
Fire emoji for "Confidence" - followed by score
Heart emoji for "Warmth" - score
Stars emoji for Charisma - score
Text message emoji for Clarity - score 

## 3. Message rules (WhatsApp sets font and colour)
Bold: only the overall score and the label "Better version"
Length: max 7 lines per message; one message = one job
Emojis: 1 emoji per score, none in the better version
Numbers: always out of 100, e.g. "Clarity 72/100"

## 4. Messages
Intro: for picking a situation. Top to bottom: one line on what this does, the four situations as numbered options. Main action: reply 1-4 -> the demo person's opener (party: a prompt for the user to start)
Scorecard + Better version: for showing where they stand. Top to bottom: overall score in bold, four scores, "Better version" in bold, their rewritten words. Main action: answer the follow-up
Follow-up: one question from the demo person, in character
Missing fact: asks only for the one missing fact, e.g. "What hike % are you asking for?"
Loading: "Listening to your answer..."
Error (voice note can't be read): "I couldn't hear that clearly. Send it again?"
Done (85+ held to the end): "You're ready. Here's your final version:" + their best version
Stop / 2 min silence: "Here's your best version so far:" + their best version

## 5. The first message's words (except for "Speak up at a Party)
Headline: Practise your hard conversation before it happens.
Under it: Pick one. The other person speaks first (at the party, you do), you reply with a voice note, and you get a score and the exact words to say.
Options: 1 Ask for a raise · 2 Explain a work delay · 3 Speak up at a party · 4 Clarity in your relationship

## 6. Principles
- One message does one job.
- The demo person always stays in character; scores come in a separate message.
- Never vague: every piece of feedback comes with the exact words to say.
Scorecard: keep overall, Clarity, Confidence, Charisma, Warmth in order with existing bold and emojis. Add one Heard: line after Warmth, then scored from: audio. Better version remains a separate message.

Missing-fact clarification: vague goals receive `What specific goal had we agreed on?`; vague outcomes receive `What specific outcome did you deliver?`. Keep one question per message and stay in the manager's character.

Heard: one line after Warmth with the Convex-counted fillers, long pauses and hedges from word timestamps. Keep the existing seventh source line, order, bolding and emojis. Do not show AI-estimated counts or pitch.
