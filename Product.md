# PRODUCT.md

## 1. The job
Just before a high stakes conversation, I want to communicate the right thing, so that I don't end up losing the conversation.
Other moments it happens: Asking for a raise, joining a room at a party, asking the person I'm dating where this is going.
Who, by situation (not age, city or title): Someone prepping for an important conversation that they don't want to get wrong. They either don't have anyone to rehearse with or feel embarrassed to talk about it with their friends.
Today they hire: A chatbot, a friend's advice, or nothing

(Advanced, optional) How they want to feel: Satisfied, peace of mind that they said what they actually feel without losing out on their interpersonal relationship.
(Advanced, optional) How they want to look to others: Sincere, Credible and Relevant.

## 2. The switch
What they'd fire: ChatGPT, a friend's advice, or nothing

The forces that matter:
Push, outside them: ChatGPT gives just a template, can't hear tone, gives vague feedback and doesn't ask the follow-up. Too many steps, and no direct audio feedback.
Pull, inside them: They know exactly what to say and how to answer the likely follow-ups.
Anxiety: Another ChatGPT giving vague advice.
Habit: ChatGPT is open and free; a friend is one message away.

What the product does about each: 
Push -> Scores the voice note on Clarity, Confidence, Charisma and Warmth, gives a better version of their own words, and asks the follow-up.
Pull -> The demo person keeps asking follow-ups until the overall score stays above 85, so they know what to say and how to answer what comes next.
Anxiety -> After one voice note they get a score and the exact words to say, not vague advice.
Habit -> It lives in WhatsApp, with no login, no name and no setup prompt. The demo person speaks first except for one situation laid out below

## 3. The core flow
Today (what they do now, before your product):
1. A high stakes conversation is coming up.
2. They think of asking a friend, but feel embarrassed to bring it up.
3. They record themselves having the conversation.
4. Save the recording.
5. Upload the recording to ChatGPT.
6. Get the feedback.
7. The feedback does not help with the details.
8. It does not make them better for the next time.
9. They feel frustrated.
[where it ends today] They don't rehearse, and walk into the conversation unprepared.

With my product (same flow for every scenario):
1. They have a hard conversation coming up and aren't sure how to say it.
2. They open WhatsApp and go to the practice chat.
3. They pick the use case (asking for a raise, explain your work delay, speak up at a party, Get clarity on your relationship situation.
4. The app asks different things based on scenario selected and they send one voice note.
Scenario: 
Asking for a raise: I have 2 mins before my next meeting - what are you asking on pay?
Explain a work delay: What's the status of the task we discussed?
Speak up at a party where the host is busy: The host is busy, so there is no first conversation by the app. The user has to be prompted to say something to start the conversation 
Get clarity on your relationship situation: Can we talk about us for a minute? 
5. The app gives a score and a better version.
6. The demo person asks a follow-up based on what they said, and they answer again, until they score 85+.
[last] They know what to say and how to answer the follow-ups.

Score: Clarity, Confidence, Charisma, Warmth, each out of 100. Overall = the average of the four. Done when the overall stays above 85 to the end of the conversation.
Clarity: Is the main point clear in the first 2 sentences of the ask?
Charisma: How well can you influence the other person based on the conversation you have?
Warmth: How respectful are you to the other person irrespective of situations and disagreements?
Confidence: How much do you hold your ground through repeated pushbacks and scrutiny?

Things it takes to get the job done today: 9 · With my product: 6

What must not happen:
Step 4: A fact is missing (e.g. the date). The app asks only for that fact.
Step 6: They quit before 85. "stop" sends the best version immediately; 2 minutes of silence sends the best version.
Raise: the review date is unknown. The app asks for the month and reminds them on the 1st.

Other flows (stop when the job is covered): The same flow for each situation:
- Ask for a raise (demo manager): hike % you want, why you deserve it, outcome you delivered, performance goals agreed, over or under achieved them.
- Explain a work delay (manager or client, 10 minutes before the 1:1): the one main reason, new date and time, % done.
- Join the room at a party (The host is busy and the user has to open the conversation either with the first person standing alone, just off a conversation or scrolling their phone; on the way or at the entrance): know people or first time, why you're attending, anything in common.
- Dating, where is this going (your partner, a few hours before): how long you've been seeing each other, how serious it is, what you want them to feel about you.

## 4. Onboarding
First value (the moment it first does the job for them): After their first voice note, they get an instant score and a better version of what they said, one they can speak as-is in the real conversation.
The smallest commitment we ask for: One voice note answer. Nothing more.
The worry it removes (from section 2): That it's another ChatGPT giving vague advice.

From opening the link to the first value:
1. Open the WhatsApp chat. removes: any login, no need to give their name
2. Pick the situation: ask for a raise, explain a delay, find your place at a party, know where we stand in dating. removes: the worry that it's just like ChatGPT
3. The demo person speaks first (e.g. demo manager: "I have 2 mins before my next meeting, what are you asking for on pay?") except the party: the app prompts them to start. removes: the doubt that it won't feel like the real conversation
4. They answer with a voice note and get the score and a better version. [first value] removes: the worry that the answer won't work in real life

Login: Not in v1. Their WhatsApp number is enough.
What we don't ask on day one: name, profile.
What we ask later, and when: the promotion date, after their first raise session.

## 5. v1
Does (the must haves: one person finishes the one job):
1. Four situations to pick from, each with its own demo person and the facts the app checks for (listed under Other flows).
2. The demo person speaks first. The user answers with a voice note except the party: the app prompts them to start
3. If a fact is missing, the app asks only for that fact.
4. A score on Clarity, Confidence, Charisma and Warmth, each out of 100. Overall = the average.
5. A better version of their own words, usable as-is in the real conversation.
6. The demo person asks follow-ups until the overall score stays above 85.
7. "stop", or 2 minutes of silence, sends their best version so far.
Doesn't (not this sprint: parked, not forgotten): Calendar access (the user tells the app the meeting date/time instead)
Nice to have (only after the must haves work): The promotion date reminder, or a reminder an hour before the actual conversation so they can practise just before doing it live
How I'll know it worked (what they do again, not what they say): They come back and use it for a different situation, or redo the same situation, within 7 days. The app checks with them on whether it worked in the real situation. They might also refer the app to someone else.

## 6. The riskiest guess
If this is false, the product is pointless: 
People pick this over ChatGPT voice plus a reminder.
The AI can actually hear confidence and warmth in a voice note.

Thirty-minute check, no code (run it while you build), and what happened: Uploaded my voice note to ChatGPT; it said audio playback and transcription weren't available in that session, so it couldn't listen. Not proven yet. Milestone 1 tests it on the build.

##7. Milestones
1. I can send one voice note answer to the demo manager's raise question and get back scores for Clarity, Confidence, Charisma and Warmth, plus a better version of my own words.
2. I can keep answering the demo manager's follow-ups until my overall score stays above 85, and "stop" or 2 minutes of silence sends me my best version.
3. I can leave out a fact (e.g. the hike %) and the app asks me only for that fact.
4. I can do the same in the delay, party (where I start the conversation) and dating scenarios.
5. I can do all of this inside WhatsApp: open the chat, pick a situation, practise.
Last: I can close it, reopen it, and my data is still there.
